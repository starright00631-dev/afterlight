Light · model and scientific references [source-afdea3a1-1b70-4b62-8fd8-9f3a1d142d52, p-1-ssdsxe]
For an ideal point lamp above a horizontal detector, E = I*h/(dx²+dy²+h²)^(3/2). Directly below the lamp, E = I/h². Contributions from independent clear lamps add. Opaque vertical screens block a ray that intersects at or below their top.
